# ED-02: Defer final technology-stack selection to Milestone 2

**Status:** Accepted (Milestone 1)

## Context

We could try to pick our final technology stack now, at Milestone 1.

## Alternatives considered

- Decide the technology stack now
- Deliberately defer the decision to Milestone 2

## Decision

Deferred. Final technology-stack selection is left for M2.

## Rationale

We don't have enough evidence yet on hosting options (see FEC-01 in [Forward Engineering Considerations](../PED/forward-engineering-considerations.md)).

## Trade-offs / risks

Keeps our options open, but means we need to make sure this gets decided properly in M2 (informed by the A2 research), not rushed.
